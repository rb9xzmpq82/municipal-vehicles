## FAQ

**Q: how to become a toy engineer**

A special truck operates on a standard truck chassis with a power take-off (PTO) driving the specialized equipment: hydraulics raise, lower or drive the working body while the operator controls the cycle from the cab or a panel. Daily checks cover PTO engagement, hydraulic oil level and body mounting bolts. Export units usually run a 260 hp diesel engine matched to the body's demand, keeping fuel burn predictable at roughly 18-25 L/100 km depending on load.

**Q: what is a lowboy gooseneck trailer**

A lowboy gooseneck trailer is a type of heavy-duty trailer designed to transport oversized and very heavy equipment, such as excavators, bulldozers, and construction machinery. It features a low deck height, which gives it a lower center of gravity and allows for hauling taller loads while staying within road height limits. The “gooseneck” connects to the towing truck at a raised point over the bed, improving stability and weight distribution. This trailer is commonly used in construction, agriculture, and industrial logistics. Depending on size, capacity, and brand, a lowboy gooseneck trailer typically costs around USD 25,000 to USD 90,000.

**Q: what is the largest car carrier ship**

In practical terms, a car carrier is a towing and recovery truck where the body and the chassis are engineered together: frame reinforcement, power take-off, hydraulics and controls all match the workload. Buyers in Africa, the Middle East and Southeast Asia import these units directly from Chinese factories at $28,000-85,000 FOB, saving 30-40% versus local dealer prices while getting a new, warrantied vehicle.
