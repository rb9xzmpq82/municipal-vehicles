For buyers importing a box truck for sale in Vietnam from China, the recommended process is: (1) obtain formal quotations from at least three verified manufacturers, (2) arrange third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, BV, or TUV), (3) use 30% T/T deposit with balance against shipping documents, and (4) confirm written warranty terms including claim response times. ## Field Notes

A typical new 3.5-ton to 8-ton box truck from China may cost around USD 18,000 to 45,000, depending on chassis brand, cargo box length, insulation level, and refrigeration equipment. Refrigerated versions are usually higher, often USD 28,000 to 65,000 or more, especially when fitted with a reliable cooling unit and thicker insulation panels. Common specifications include payloads from 2 to 10 tons, box lengths from 3.8 to 7.8 meters, diesel engines, and manual or automatic transmissions. For special-duty applications, buyers sometimes also ask about items like a “logger box truck bed for sale,” but for overseas imports it is important to confirm whether the truck body is for general cargo, frozen food, or a specialized industry.

For example, a 4.2-meter box truck with a 4x2 diesel chassis may be suitable for urban delivery, while a 6.8-meter refrigerated unit is better for wholesale food distribution. The key is to match the truck to payload, route distance, climate, and maintenance capacity. With a careful supplier choice and a clear order process, importing a box truck from China can be a cost-effective and reliable solution for overseas buyers.
