## FAQ

**Q: how much are tow trucks to own**

For most overseas buyers a tow truck lands between $12,500 and $245,000 FOB China. The low end usually means a standard 338 hp Chinese chassis with manual gearbox; the top end covers premium chassis and larger capacity. Payment is commonly 30% T/T deposit with balance before shipment, and lead time runs 30-60 days after deposit.

**Q: how many boxes fit in a 26 foot truck**

A 26-foot truck typically holds about 1,500 to 2,200 standard moving boxes, depending on box size, how they are packed, and whether they are stacked efficiently. For example, if you use mostly medium boxes, you may fit closer to the middle of that range. Larger boxes or irregularly shaped items will reduce the total. Weight distribution also matters, since a truck must be loaded safely and evenly. If you share the box dimensions and whether the load includes furniture or pallets, I can estimate the capacity more accurately.

**Q: what are the best heavy duty trucks**

Start with three numbers: daily workload, required capacity and road conditions. Then pick the chassis — 4x2 for city routes, 6x4 or 8x4 for heavy loads and rough sites. For a special truck, a 260 hp engine with a special purpose body is the common export configuration. Verify emission compliance for your country (Euro 3/4/5 for most African and Asian markets), ask for factory inspection photos before container loading, and reserve 10-15% of the truck price for first-year spare parts.

**Q: what is the largest car carrier ship**

In practical terms, a car carrier is a towing and recovery truck where the body and the chassis are engineered together: frame reinforcement, power take-off, hydraulics and controls all match the workload. Buyers in Africa, the Middle East and Southeast Asia import these units directly from Chinese factories at $28,000-85,000 FOB, saving 30-40% versus local dealer prices while getting a new, warrantied vehicle.
