**Q: what are armored fighting vehicles**

A special truck is a purpose-built special purpose vehicle mounted on a truck chassis, engineered for daily commercial duty rather than general haulage. Chinese export versions typically use Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW or ISUZU chassis with a specialized body, and are supplied with FOB pricing, CE or destination-market certificates and 12-month warranties. **Q: how does a golf cart engine work**

Mechanically, a special truck is a chassis plus engineered body: the engine drives both the wheels and, through the PTO, the working system. Control is electro-hydraulic, with safety interlocks that stop the body when the truck moves. Chinese export models are designed for simple maintenance — most service points are reachable with standard tools, and factories ship a spare kit (filters, seals, hoses) with each unit.
