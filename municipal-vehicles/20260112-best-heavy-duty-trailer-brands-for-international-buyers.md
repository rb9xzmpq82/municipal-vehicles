## FAQ

**Q: how much does it cost to tow a car 3 miles**

Budget $28,000-85,000 FOB China for a special truck, with the final figure set by capacity tier and chassis choice. Most buyers pick a Chinese chassis (Dongfeng, HOWO, FOTON) for value; Japanese and European options raise the price but improve resale. Do not forget landed costs: freight, import duty and pre-shipment inspection typically add 20-35% on top of FOB. Request itemized quotations from at least three factories to compare fairly.

Mechanically, a special truck is a chassis plus engineered body: the engine drives both the wheels and, through the PTO, the working system. Chinese export models are designed for simple maintenance — most service points are reachable with standard tools, and factories ship a spare kit (filters, seals, hoses) with each unit.

**Q: how to box truck**

To box a truck, you typically need to convert a chassis cab or flatbed into an enclosed cargo body. The process includes selecting the right box size and material, building or installing the cargo box, adding a rear roll-up or swing door, reinforcing the frame, and making sure the suspension, brakes, and payload capacity match the new load. Many buyers choose a custom upfit from a truck body manufacturer or dealer. If you need a complete box truck conversion, pricing usually ranges from about USD 15,000 to 45,000 depending on truck size, materials, and features. Always confirm local registration and compliance requirements before installation.
