# Garbage Truck Driver Jobs: Specs, Pricing and Import Guide

> 垃圾车 driver jobs

Applications for the garbage truck driver jobs span municipal services, construction sites, industrial facilities and agricultural operations. In African markets, government and NGO procurement drives significant demand, while in Southeast Asia the private construction sector is the primary buyer. Latin American buyers increasingly source directly from Chinese factories to bypass import markup layers.

The garbage truck driver jobs is typically configured with a power take-off (PTO) system driving the specialized equipment. Engine options range from 180 to 420 hp depending on capacity requirements, with 6-speed to 12-speed manual or AMT gearboxes. Emission standards range from Euro 3 to Euro 6 depending on the destination market's requirements.

For buyers importing a garbage truck driver jobs from China, the recommended process is: (1) obtain formal quotations from at least three verified manufacturers, (2) arrange third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, BV, or TUV), (3) use 30% T/T deposit with balance against shipping documents, and (4) confirm written warranty terms including claim response times. This keeps risk low and ensures a clean paper trail for customs clearance.

## Field Notes

A frequent mistake is buying a used rear load garbage truck for sale without checking corrosion inside the hopper, hydraulic leaks, and PTO condition. Another is ignoring local service capability. If spare parts must be imported for every repair, the truck’s uptime will drop quickly. For municipal fleets, a well-specified truck from a reputable Chinese factory often delivers better total value than a low-priced unit with uncertain history.

In short, overseas buyers importing from China should focus on working-spec vehicles, transparent quotations, and ownership cost over three to five years. That approach is usually the difference between a truck that merely arrives and a truck that actually performs on the street.

**Q: what is a car carrier truck called**

A car carrier truck is commonly called a **car carrier**, **auto transporter**, or **vehicle transport truck**. In the trucking and logistics industry, it is often referred to as a **car hauler**. These trucks are designed to transport multiple vehicles at once, usually on a specialized trailer with one or two levels. For single-vehicle transport, some people may also use the term **rollback tow truck** or **flatbed tow truck**, depending on the design. If you are sourcing this type of truck for commercial use, the exact name can vary by configuration and capacity, but “car carrier truck” and “auto transporter” are the most widely used terms.
