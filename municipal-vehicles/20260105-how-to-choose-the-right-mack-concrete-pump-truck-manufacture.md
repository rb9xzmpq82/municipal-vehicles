## Field Notes

A concrete pump truck is a specialized construction vehicle designed to transport and place concrete efficiently in high-rise buildings, bridges, tunnels, foundations, and other demanding job sites. For overseas buyers importing special vehicles from China, this category typically includes three main configurations: tanker-type pump trucks, semi-trailer concrete pumps, and mixer pump trucks. Each model serves a different workflow, but all are valued for faster pouring, reduced labor, and improved concrete placement accuracy.

Concrete pump trucks are widely used in commercial buildings, housing projects, roadworks, ports, and industrial plants. Boom trucks are ideal for reaching elevated or hard-to-access pouring points. Semi-trailer pumps are often deployed for dams, tunnels, and large foundations where long continuous pumping is needed. Mixer pump trucks are especially useful for farm construction, village roads, and dispersed job sites where separate batching and pumping equipment would be inefficient.
