## FAQ

**Q: how many boxes fit in a 26 foot truck**

A 26-foot truck typically holds about 1,500 to 2,200 standard moving boxes, depending on box size, how they are packed, and whether they are stacked efficiently. For example, if you use mostly medium boxes, you may fit closer to the middle of that range. Larger boxes or irregularly shaped items will reduce the total. Weight distribution also matters, since a truck must be loaded safely and evenly.
