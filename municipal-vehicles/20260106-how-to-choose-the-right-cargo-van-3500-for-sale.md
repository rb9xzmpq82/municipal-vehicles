# How to Choose the Right Cargo Van 3500 For Sale

> 厢式货车 3500 出售

For buyers importing a cargo van 3500 for sale from China, the recommended process is: (1) obtain formal quotations from at least three verified manufacturers, (2) arrange third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, BV, or TUV), (3) use 30% T/T deposit with balance against shipping documents, and (4) confirm written warranty terms including claim response times. This keeps risk low and ensures a clean paper trail for customs clearance.

Pricing for a cargo van 3500 for sale varies significantly based on chassis origin, body capacity, and specification level. Chinese chassis builds offer the best value, while Japanese (ISUZU, Hino) and European (MAN, Volvo, Scania) options command premiums of 25% to 65%. Ocean freight by RO-RO or container adds $3,000 to $8,000 depending on destination port.

Applications for the cargo van 3500 for sale span municipal services, construction sites, industrial facilities and agricultural operations. In African markets, government and NGO procurement drives significant demand, while in Southeast Asia the private construction sector is the primary buyer. Latin American buyers increasingly source directly from Chinese factories to bypass import markup layers.

**Q: how long is 5 ton truck**

Mechanically, a special truck is a chassis plus engineered body: the engine drives both the wheels and, through the PTO, the working system. Control is electro-hydraulic, with safety interlocks that stop the body when the truck moves. Chinese export models are designed for simple maintenance — most service points are reachable with standard tools, and factories ship a spare kit (filters, seals, hoses) with each unit.
