## Field Notes

A standard wing van truck with aluminum side wings and a box body is often used for general freight, retail distribution, and factory delivery. Typical cargo bodies range from 4.2 m to 9.6 m, with payloads from 3 to 18 tons depending on chassis class. For refrigerated work, buyers should pay closer attention to insulation thickness, door sealing, refrigeration unit brand, and the local ambient temperature profile. A common export spec is 50 to 80 mm polyurethane insulation, which is usually enough for chilled goods; frozen cargo may require stronger insulation and a more capable unit.

For refrigerated imports, also compare compressor power, standby option, and insulation performance at high outside temperatures. In tropical markets, a unit that works well in mild weather may struggle at 38 to 45 degrees Celsius. That leads to product loss, higher diesel use, and more service calls. A good refrigerated wing van truck may add USD 3,000 to 10,000 over a basic box body, but the cold-chain protection can justify the difference quickly.
