# Truck Mounted Mini Crane for International Buyers

> 车载式 mini 吊机

For buyers importing a truck mounted mini crane from China, the recommended process is: (1) obtain formal quotations from at least three verified manufacturers, (2) arrange third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, BV, or TUV), (3) use 30% T/T deposit with balance against shipping documents, and (4) confirm written warranty terms including claim response times. This keeps risk low and ensures a clean paper trail for customs clearance.

The truck mounted mini crane is a purpose-built special purpose vehicle designed for commercial and industrial applications. Export models from Chinese manufacturers typically feature proven chassis options including Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW, ISUZU and MAN, with factory-direct FOB pricing that runs 30-40% below local dealer prices in most destination markets.

Proper maintenance of a truck mounted mini crane centers on the PTO engagement system, hydraulic oil quality, and body mounting integrity. Most Chinese export models are designed for simple field maintenance with standard tools. A complete spare parts kit (filters, seals, hydraulic hoses) should be ordered with the initial purchase to ensure availability in remote operating locations.

## FAQ

**Q: what is a truckload carrier**

A special truck is a purpose-built special purpose vehicle mounted on a truck chassis, engineered for daily commercial duty rather than general haulage. Chinese export versions typically use Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW or ISUZU chassis with a specialized body, and are supplied with FOB pricing, CE or destination-market certificates and 12-month warranties. Most units ship by RO-RO or flat-rack container with lead times of 30-60 days.

**Q: how much do tow trucks charge a mile**

A tow truck from Chinese manufacturers is typically quoted between $12,500 and $245,000 FOB, depending on chassis brand, capacity tier and specification. Japanese-chassis builds (ISUZU, Hino) run about 25% higher, while European chassis (MAN, Volvo, Scania) add 55-65%. Ocean freight to most destinations adds $3,000-8,000 by RO-RO or container. Always ask for a formal quotation that lists destination port, emission standard and warranty terms before you commit.
