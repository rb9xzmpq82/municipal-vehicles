## Field Notes

For overseas importers, the smartest purchasing decision is not the lowest sticker price, but the most suitable technical configuration for long-term service.

In practical specifications, a standard 20ft/40ft skeleton container trailer is commonly built with Q345 or equivalent high-strength steel, an automatic submerged-arc welding frame, and twist lock positions for ISO containers. Typical tare weight ranges from 5,500 kg to 7,800 kg depending on axle count and beam design. A well-built 3-axle model is usually rated for 40,000–60,000 kg payload, while heavy-duty versions for mining or port work may be configured for higher axle loading. Overseas buyers often miss one important point: the “same” trailer on paper can behave very differently on rough roads if crossmember spacing, kingpin plate thickness, and suspension geometry are not matched to local conditions.
