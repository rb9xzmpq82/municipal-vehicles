# Used Hino Refrigerated Trucks For Sale: Specs, Pricing and Import Guide

> 二手 日野 冷藏 卡车 出售

For buyers importing a used hino refrigerated trucks for sale from China, the recommended process is: (1) obtain formal quotations from at least three verified manufacturers, (2) arrange third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, BV, or TUV), (3) use 30% T/T deposit with balance against shipping documents, and (4) confirm written warranty terms including claim response times. This keeps risk low and ensures a clean paper trail for customs clearance.

Pricing for a used hino refrigerated trucks for sale varies significantly based on chassis origin, body capacity, and specification level. Chinese chassis builds offer the best value, while Japanese (ISUZU, Hino) and European (MAN, Volvo, Scania) options command premiums of 25% to 65%. Ocean freight by RO-RO or container adds $3,000 to $8,000 depending on destination port.

The used hino refrigerated trucks for sale is typically configured with a power take-off (PTO) system driving the specialized equipment. Engine options range from 180 to 420 hp depending on capacity requirements, with 6-speed to 12-speed manual or AMT gearboxes. Emission standards range from Euro 3 to Euro 6 depending on the destination market's requirements.

## FAQ

**Q: 1. What B-Series models are available for Texas buyers?**

The B-Series runs from the B3 (23 feet) through the B8 (28 feet). The B4 and B5 are available in both ICE and electric configurations. The B3 and B8 are currently ICE only. Endera's sales team can confirm current in-stock availability and lead times for Texas delivery.

**Q: what is a side tipper truck**

In practical terms, a tipper truck is a tanker and trailer truck where the body and the chassis are engineered together: frame reinforcement, power take-off, hydraulics and controls all match the workload. Buyers in Africa, the Middle East and Southeast Asia import these units directly from Chinese factories at $14,000-198,000 FOB, saving 30-40% versus local dealer prices while getting a new, warrantied vehicle.

**Q: what is emergency care management**

A special truck is a purpose-built special purpose vehicle mounted on a truck chassis, engineered for daily commercial duty rather than general haulage. Chinese export versions typically use Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW or ISUZU chassis with a specialized body, and are supplied with FOB pricing, CE or destination-market certificates and 12-month warranties. Most units ship by RO-RO or flat-rack container with lead times of 30-60 days.
