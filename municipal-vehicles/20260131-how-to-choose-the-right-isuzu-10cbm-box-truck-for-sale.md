## Field Notes

When buyers ask about **box truck standard sizes**, the most common cargo body lengths in export projects are 4.2 m, 5.1 m, 6.2 m, 7.6 m, and 9.6 m. Smaller units suit urban delivery, while larger models are preferred for regional logistics and long-haul distribution. A 4.2 m box truck typically carries around 1.5 to 3 tons, while a 6.2 m model often handles 5 to 8 tons depending on chassis configuration.

For overseas buyers, the best variant depends on cargo type and operating climate. If the goods are frozen or chilled, choose a refrigerated box truck with a stronger compressor, better insulation, and a chassis that can support the added weight. If the goal is general cargo transport, a dry box body truck offers lower purchase price, simpler structure, and lower maintenance cost.
